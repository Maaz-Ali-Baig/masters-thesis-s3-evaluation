configuration of this deployment, not necessarily because RustFS cannot do SSE-S3.

run). NOT PROVEN: that the data is encrypted at rest (the probe tests the API only). The reasons for C19 and C21 were not examined again here.

PROVEN: RustFS 1.0.1 rejects every unsigned header case (p2 to p8) with AccessDenied, on three nodes and on one node, while the 1.0.0-beta.8 image
accepted all of them on one node. The difference is caused by the version (same topology comparison for 1.0.1, same single node setup for the two
versions). Not examined: which change in the source of RustFS did this (the release notes of 1.0.1 mention the handling of presigned requests, this was
not matched to the test).
CONSEQUENCE for the thesis: the RustFS result of 2 October (unsigned headers accepted) belongs to 1.0.0-beta.8 and must be labelled with that
version. The VM runs and every later RustFS result are for 1.0.1.
