DEVIATIONS FROM THE PROTOCOL (kept in the record)
-------------------------------------------------
D1. The "ACTION NOW" stop line was printed at 15:48:49 but both VMs were stopped only at about 15:54:13 (the script waited 324 s for the user; my reminder to the user came late). This does not change the outage
    length, which starts at the silence seen by the probe, but the baseline before the stop was longer than the planned 300 s.
D2. The planned outage of 360 s was exceeded. The script reads the seed set and the acknowledged objects through the endpoint of ceph0 during the outage, and that read-back ran from 15:55:13 until about
    16:05:48 (reads against a cluster without monitor quorum wait for their client timeouts); the script prints "power on" only after it. At 16:02:47 the read-back was still running and I asked the user to power
    on both VMs without waiting for the prompt (the user confirmed; the exact click time was not read). The monitors answered again in the repair watch at 16:04:19, so the outage lasted from 15:54:13 to roughly
    16:03 to 16:04, about 9 to 10 minutes instead of 6. The "ssh answers" lines of the script (16:05:48) are therefore NOT the boot times of the nodes. The same effect made the Garage S2 outage longer (garage record).
D3. Because of D2 the read-back "during the outage" ran partly after the power on: of 350 objects read through ceph0, 132 were ERROR_OR_EMPTY and 218 OK. That split is a TIMING artefact (reads before the monitors
    returned failed, later reads succeeded), not a property of the objects, and is NOT used as a result. The timestamped probe (head_object of the fixed object every few seconds) is used instead.

PROVEN
------
G1. With two of three nodes stopped no acknowledged write happened for 657.7 s: the failed attempts (33) lie between 15:54:26 and 16:04:28 and the writes resumed at about 16:04:56 after the return of the nodes.
G2. The monitors had no quorum from the first repair watch row after the silence (NOQUORUM at 15:54:17; the row before, 15:53:56, was HEALTH_OK) and the status command did not answer until 16:04:19.
G3. The one surviving node (ceph0) did not serve reads of the fixed object from 15:55:20 and its S3 endpoint stopped answering altogether from 16:00:00 to 16:04:58 (the probe got no HTTP answer). Why the endpoint
    went silent 6 minutes after the loss is OPEN (HYPOTHESIS: the RGW of ceph0 stopped serving without a monitor quorum; test: read the RGW log and its state at that time, not done).
G4. After the power on of both nodes the service came back without any command on the nodes (the cephadm target is enabled): the endpoints answered again within about 3 s of each other at 16:04:55 to 16:04:58 and the
    cluster was HEALTH_OK at 16:05:48. The time from the boot to the first acknowledged write cannot be given, because the click time was not read; counted from the first row of the repair watch with
    monitor quorum (16:04:19) the first acknowledged write came about 37 s later (16:04:56).
G5. No acknowledged write was lost: 324 of 324 readable on all three endpoints, and the 45 seed objects too.
